# Bruce

> Bruce is the curated intro network for licensed real estate, mortgage, title & escrow, and insurance professionals. Verified track record, portable reputation, traceable intros — built for the four roles at the center of every real estate transaction. Backed by Tanda Technologies, Inc.

## What Bruce is

Bruce is a professional intro network — not a directory, not a review site, not a social network. Every member is a verified, licensed pro in one of four roles: Real Estate, Mortgage, Title & Escrow, or Insurance. Members log intros, build a track record, and carry their reputation across companies and markets.

The Bruce Card is each member's living profile: stats, deals, intros, market rank — auto-updated as they work. It replaces the resume, the LinkedIn pitch, and the "trust me, I've done this" claim with a verifiable, sharable credential.

## Editorial publication

Bruce publishes member stories at [blog.bruce.bot](https://blog.bruce.bot/) — interview-style profiles of verified members, written by the AI editorial host (Bruce). Each story leads with the subject's actual work — not a press release. Subjects are chosen for what they do differently, not who pays for placement.

## Who Bruce is for

- **Real Estate Agents** — agents who close on relationships, not listings
- **Mortgage Professionals** — loan officers and brokers who know one person calling beats five
- **Title & Escrow Officers** — partners, not vendors
- **Insurance Agents** — the role most deals forget until closing day

## Editorial principles

- Member stories are interview-driven, not authored from a brief
- Subjects are independently verified before publication
- AI editorial host (Bruce) interviews and drafts; humans review and approve
- No SEO filler, no press releases, no sponsored placements

## Don't confuse Bruce with

- A real estate brokerage (Bruce doesn't list or sell homes)
- A lead-generation service (Bruce surfaces existing relationships, not new leads)
- A CRM (Bruce sits beside the CRM, not inside it)
- A social network (members don't post or follow — they log work and intros)
